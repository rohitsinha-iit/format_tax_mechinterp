## EXPLORATORY (not pre-registered): calibration of the §8.4.3 sign-flip null

The pre-registered null flips the sign of each JS−FF paired difference d_i. Because f is estimated very reliably (split-half cos ≈ 0.999), f_null = s̄·f + mean_i s_i(d_i − f), and the small leftover term s̄·f (|s̄| ≈ 1/√n) still dominates f_null's *direction*. A cosine is scale-invariant, so the null largely reproduces ±cos(f⊥c, b) and its p99 tracks the observed value at every layer.
